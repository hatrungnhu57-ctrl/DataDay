# Privacy Policy for DataDay

**Last updated:** October 3, 2026

DataDay ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage.

---

## 1. Data Collection & Privacy First
- **No Personal Data Collected:** DataDay does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, phone number, or location.
- **On-Device Data Processing:** All cellular data usage statistics, Wi-Fi usage, speed test metrics, and love counter calculations are computed and stored **100% locally on your device**.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide any personal details to use any features of DataDay.

---

## 2. Permissions & How They Are Used
- **Photos / Photo Library:** Used solely when you choose to customize your love widget background or crop a photo with your partner. Selected photos remain entirely on your device and are never uploaded to any server.
- **Photo Library Additions (Save Photo):** Used solely when you tap to export and save a SpeedTest result card to your Photos app.
- **Network / Internet Access:** Used exclusively to perform network speed tests (ping, download, upload measurements) against selected servers and to read local network interface counters.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Tracking
- DataDay **does not use VPNs**, tracking SDKs, third-party analytics trackers, or advertising networks.
- We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.
